👋 Hi, I'm Philipp — a Senior Software Engineer focused on cloud and web, with 18 years in IT and a deep background in web development, UX, and software engineering.

I design and build scalable, resilient applications across the Microsoft Azure ecosystem — combining frontend development with React and TypeScript, backend development with C#, cloud services on Azure, and infrastructure as code, delivered through DevOps practices and automation.

I'm now going deeper into cloud-native engineering: cloud architecture, containers and Kubernetes, CI/CD and GitOps, observability, and AI integration — building on the application and UX craft I've developed over nearly two decades. I like connecting frontend, backend, cloud, and platform into solutions that are technically solid, a pleasure to use, and efficient to run.

This blog is where I document real implementations, architecture ideas, experiments, and lessons learned along the way.

When I'm not exploring systems and ideas in the cloud, you'll find me spending time with my family, practicing Kung Fu, or behind a camera — the best ways to stay grounded, focused, and creative.

Always learning. Always evolving. Always in the cloud ☁️
